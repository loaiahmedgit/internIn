# Landing Page

Uses brand colors: "intern" in Deep Navy `#213248`, "In" in Primary Teal `#1BA59C` (see 10-brand-identity.md). Overall style: very white/minimal.

## Navbar

**internIn** — For Students · For Companies · How it works · Pricing — Sign in · **Get started**

## Hero

**"Experience shouldn't be required to earn experience."**

Subheading: "internIn lets students prove what they can do through realistic company work challenges — so companies can choose interns based on evidence, not just CVs."

Two CTAs: **Find internships** / **Hire interns**

Product mockup flow shown: Data Analyst Intern → Complete work challenge → Performance reviewed → Invited.

## Section 2 — The paradox

Headline: "How do you get experience when every opportunity requires experience?"

Two-column contrast:
- **Traditional hiring**: CV, past experience, GPA, connections, interview
- **internIn**: Real challenge, actual work, evidence, company review, opportunity

## Section 3 — For students

Headline: "Don't tell them what you can do. Show them."

Copy: Browse opportunities. Complete realistic challenges. Get discovered based on your work. Earn verified experience.

CTA: **Explore opportunities**

## Section 4 — For companies

Headline: "Stop guessing from CVs."

Copy: "Describe what your intern will actually do. internIn turns it into a realistic, safe work challenge." Shown via a mock AI conversation (Manager: "We're looking for a marketing intern…" → internIn AI: "What kind of work will they handle?" → generated challenge shown).

## Section 5 — AI Challenge Builder (most visually important section)

Headline: "From role to realistic assessment in minutes."

Flow: Describe your work → AI creates a safe simulation → Review & publish.

## Section 6 — Candidate evidence

Headline: "See how they actually performed."

Shows candidate cards (e.g. Ahmed, Sara, Noor) with completed tasks, time, strengths, actual submission — explicitly no giant AI score shown.

## Section 7 — After hiring

Headline: "Hiring them is only the beginning."

Copy: "Generate a structured internship program, track milestones, collect supervisor feedback and give interns verified experience when they finish." Shown as an 8-week timeline visual.

## Section 8 — Privacy

Headline: "Realistic work. Without exposing real company data."

Copy: AI can generate synthetic datasets, fictional customers, safe business scenarios, sanitized documents. Line: "Your internal systems stay private."

## Section 9 — Pricing

"Simple pricing" — Students: Free (explore internships, complete challenges, build verified experience). Companies: Free to start (create internships, generate challenges, review candidates), then QAR 499 when you hire an intern (includes internship management).

## Final CTA

Headline: "Give talent a way in."

For students: "Prove what you can do." For companies: "See what they can do." CTA: **Get started**
