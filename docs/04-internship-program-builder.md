# Internship Program Builder & Workspace

internIn does not stop at hiring — this is what makes it harder to dismiss as "just an assessment website," and it solves a second, separate company pain point: "now what do we actually make the intern do for eight weeks?"

## AI Internship Builder

After an offer is accepted, the manager describes the internship to AI in natural language, e.g.: "Ahmed is joining our marketing department for 8 weeks, 20 hours weekly. We want him to learn our product, understand competitors, work with campaign analytics and eventually contribute to a real campaign."

AI generates a week-by-week plan, e.g.:
- Week 1 — Onboarding
- Week 2 — Market research
- Week 3 — Competitor analysis
- Week 4 — Campaign analytics
- Week 5 — Campaign planning
- Week 6 — Execution support
- Week 7 — Optimization
- Week 8 — Final project

The manager can edit anything — drag, remove, edit, add tasks, learning objectives, meetings, deliverables, milestones, deadlines, supervisor reviews — or simply tell the AI ("Make week 3 less difficult") and the AI edits it. Principle: **AI proposes, the company controls.**

## Internship Workspace

**Student view**: Today, This week, Milestones, Tasks, Upcoming check-in, Feedback, Learning resources.

**Supervisor view**: Intern progress — tasks completed, submissions, feedback, milestones, blockers.

This prevents the common failure mode where HR accepts an intern and then has no structured plan for what to do with them for eight weeks.

## Verified Experience

At completion, the student receives a structured, evidence-backed record — not a generic "Certificate of Completion." Example:

**Company X — Data Analyst Internship, 8 weeks, Verified**
- Work completed: Sales analysis, Dashboard, Data cleaning, Competitor research
- Skills demonstrated: SQL, Excel, Power BI, Analysis
- Supervisor verification (and optional supervisor feedback)

Over time, this becomes the student's real, verifiable work history — the actual experience that traditional hiring wrongly demanded upfront.
