# internIn — Product Concept

internIn is an early-career hiring and internship platform where students with little or no prior experience prove themselves through realistic company work challenges. Companies describe the internship and actual work to an AI assistant, which creates a safe simulated challenge using synthetic or sanitized data. Students complete the challenge, and companies review evidence of their performance rather than relying only on CVs. Successful candidates can be invited into an internship, after which internIn generates and manages a structured internship program with tasks, milestones, supervisor feedback and verified experience. AI assists throughout the process but never makes the hiring decision.

## The core problem

Students need experience to get internships, but they need internships to get experience. Companies also struggle: a CV looks nice, but there's no way to know if the candidate can actually do the work.

## The reframe

Traditional hiring: CV → Apply → "you need experience" → rejected.

internIn: Internship role → realistic work challenge → student proves ability → company reviews evidence → selects intern → manages internship → verified experience.

## Core principle

AI does not hire people. AI helps companies create realistic evaluations and understand candidate performance. The company decides.

## What internIn is NOT

Not LinkedIn. Not Wohaj. Not Indeed. Not an internship board. Not Tasklink. Not Upwork for students. Not HackerRank alone. Not an LMS. Not just an AI CV tool.

internIn combines work-sample hiring + early-career talent discovery + internship management.

## The moat

AI itself is not the moat — anyone can generate an assessment with an LLM. The long-term moat is the network and the data: thousands of students demonstrating real skills, evidence instead of self-declared skills, and an emerging understanding of which challenge performance predicts internship success. Plus company relationships and a large library of high-quality work simulations.

## The pitch

"We replace 'you need experience' with 'show us what you can do.'"

## The vision

Make experience no longer a prerequisite for getting your first experience. A student shouldn't lose an opportunity because another student had connections, a prior internship, or a better-looking CV. Give both the same problem, let them show what they can actually do, and let the company decide.

## Tagline (brand)

"Connecting ambition with opportunity."
