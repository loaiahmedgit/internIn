# Candidate Evidence & Comparison

## Candidate Evidence page

This page matters enormously — it is the alternative to a bare AI score. Do NOT show something like "Ahmed — 94/100 ⭐ Hire!" That is explicitly rejected as a design anti-pattern.

Instead, show factual performance evidence:
- Completed: 5/6 tasks
- Time: 71 min
- Submission: Dashboard + PDF
- Insights found: 4/5 expected themes
- Revisions: 2
- AI usage: Allowed
- Company rubric: [rubric criteria]

Plus an **AI summary** (descriptive, not a score), e.g.: "Ahmed demonstrated strong data interpretation and noticed the regional sales anomaly that most submissions missed. His dashboard was clear, although his recommendations lacked financial justification."

And a **View original work** link to the actual submission. The company sees the evidence and decides — AI never makes the hiring call.

## Candidate Comparison

A potential killer feature: managers cannot manually digest large numbers of submissions, so AI summarizes and tabulates them for comparison.

Manager selects candidates (e.g. Ahmed, Sara, Mohamed, Noor) and gets a comparison table across dimensions like Completion, Time, Analysis, Communication, Main strength, Main weakness.

From the comparison view, the manager can: Shortlist, Request interview, or Invite to Internship.

## Invite to Internship (the defining moment)

Instead of a generic "Apply," the pivotal UI moment is the company clicking **Invite to Internship** — this is where proof converts into opportunity, and is the point where internIn breaks the "CV loop" (CV → Apply → "need experience" → rejected).
