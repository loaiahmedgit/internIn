# internIn

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Companies hiring interns: recruiters, hiring managers, department managers, founders and administrators. Access is based on permissions, not job titles. Students apply with a profile, CV and challenge evidence.

## Product Purpose

Help companies manage internship postings and review applicant evidence. Human reviewers own hiring decisions; AI organizes evidence and does not rank unrelated candidates or decide whom to hire.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The Hiring Workspace contains Home, Internships, Candidates, Analytics, Integrations and Settings. Home is operational; Analytics measures hiring outcomes. Program management is a future separate experience, and its existing records and backend must remain intact.

Candidate profiles remain full pages with Overview, Resume, Challenge, Notes and Activity. Archived rejected candidates retain history, export and restore access. Candidate filtering is scoped by internship.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the real logo in public/logo.png, teal branding, existing typography, sidebar dimensions, thin borders and approved minimal table/card styling. The six user-supplied August 31 references govern this work; their old navigation labels do not.

## Evidence on Hand

Supabase-backed profiles, applications, versioned challenges, submission files, offers and event history. The demonstration company has multiple seeded internship postings. Seeded completion claims are not proof of evaluated work. Missing source attribution, evaluation or scheduling data must never be invented.

## Product Principles

- One consistent underlying hiring data source across screens.
- Preserve working routes, APIs, files, candidate stages and history.
- Make operational actions clear and stage-specific.
- Explain evidence limitations and retain human review.
- Keep unfinished integrations and future program UI out of this scope.
